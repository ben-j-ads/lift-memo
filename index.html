How it works

The numbers in the memo

The lift is the difference in conversion rate between treated and control. Its range combines each group's Wilson interval with Newcombe's method. The relative lift range uses the log of the rate ratio. Incremental conversions, revenue, profit and incremental ROAS are the lift range scaled by the treated group size, the revenue per conversion and the spend.

lift = p_treated - p_control incremental conversions = lift x treated people incremental ROAS = incremental conversions x revenue per conversion / spend profit = incremental conversions x revenue per conversion x margin - spend cost per incremental conversion = spend / incremental conversions
Sample ratio check

If you planned 10% control and you got 12%, the groups were not assigned as designed, and every number above is suspect. The check compares the control share you got with the one you planned. A strong mismatch (p below 0.001) is flagged in the memo.

Read this before you send the memo

The memo is only as good as the test. It assumes random assignment, no leakage of control people into the ads, and conversions counted the same way in both groups.

It measures the test window. Effects that arrive later, or that show up in other channels, are not in it.

Read the range, not the point. The central estimate is the least likely single value. The memo leads with the range for that reason.
